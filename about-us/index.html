What we are

An Independent Alternative to the Dealer

A main dealer will only ever see one badge. An independent workshop with the right equipment sees all of them, and can tell you when a repair is worth doing and when a car is not worth the money.

Manufacturer diagnostic platforms

Each marque is read on the platform its own dealer network uses, rather than through a generic code reader that returns a number and no context. For Mercedes-Benz, that means XENTRY.

  • XENTRY
  • ISTA
  • ODIS
  • Techstream
  • GDS2
  • CONSULT-III Plus
  • wiTECH

Mechanical, body and paint under one roof

Engine, transmission, suspension and electrical bays sit alongside a body shop and a sealed paint booth. A car that needs both does not move between sites, and no one can blame the other trade.

An itemised quotation before anything is opened

You get the diagnosis, the parts and the price before work starts. If the estimate moves once something is stripped, you hear about it and decide, rather than finding out on the invoice.

Parts matched to factory specification

OEM-equivalent parts are the default and genuine parts are available on request. Which one is going on your car is confirmed with you, not chosen quietly on price.

Records kept for the next owner

Parts and procedures are documented every visit, which preserves a service history a buyer can actually read. On a luxury car that history is worth real money at resale.

Collection and delivery across Abu Dhabi

Pick-up and drop-off reach any Abu Dhabi address with a service contract, and every service includes a wash before the car goes back.

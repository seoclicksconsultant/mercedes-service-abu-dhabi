Why act early

Why Timely Oil Changes Matter

Heat is the biggest enemy of engine oil. As a general rule, the oxidation rate of oil doubles with every 10°C rise in temperature, which roughly halves its useful life. Abu Dhabi summers, long idling in traffic and turbocharged engines all push oil temperatures higher.

Oxidised oil thickens, forms sludge and loses its protective additives. That affects timing chains, camshaft adjusters and turbochargers first, which are some of the most expensive parts to repair on a Mercedes.

Leaks are also common in the UAE. A dubizzle Cars review of over 20,000 inspections in 2026 found oil sweating in about 27% of vehicles and active oil leaks in 15%. That’s why every oil change we do includes a leak check.

Intervals

How Often Should You Change Your Mercedes Oil

Your Mercedes uses ASSYST PLUS to tell you when service is due. It calculates this from time, mileage and driving conditions. In most models, Service A comes at about 16,000 km (10,000 miles) or one year, whichever comes first.

Abu Dhabi conditions are tougher than average. Long idling in traffic, short city trips and high summer temperatures all age oil faster. If your car spends most of its time in traffic or you drive an AMG, we recommend checking the oil level every few weeks and not stretching past the ASSYST reminder.

Owner tips

Tips to Protect Your Mercedes Engine Oil

  1. 01

    Follow your ASSYST reminder

    Don’t ignore or reset the service message without doing the work.

  2. 02

    Check the level regularly

    Use the instrument cluster or the onboard oil level check every few weeks.

  3. 03

    Use the exact MB approval

    The right number on the label matters more than the brand name.

  4. 04

    Let the engine warm up gently

    Avoid hard revving until the oil reaches operating temperature.

  5. 05

    Limit long idling

    In heavy traffic, oil temperature rises with little airflow to cool it.

  6. 06

    Fix leaks early

    A small sweat can turn into a leak that damages wiring and engine mounts.

  7. 07

    Keep your invoices

    Detailed records protect your warranty and your resale value.

How much does a Mercedes oil change cost in Abu Dhabi?

It depends on the engine, oil approval and quantity. A four-cylinder needs about 6 litres, while an AMG V8 needs around 9 to 10 litres. We give you a fixed quote after confirming your VIN.

How long does a Mercedes oil change take?

A standard oil and filter change usually takes about an hour. Adding other fluids, filters or a full Service A or B takes longer.

Which oil does my Mercedes need?

It depends on your engine. Most modern Mercedes engines need an oil with a specific MB 229 approval, such as 229.52 or 229.71. We confirm the exact approval from your VIN.

Can I use 5W-30 instead of 0W-20 in my Mercedes?

Only if your engine’s approval list allows it. Some engines accept both, while newer engines may require 0W-20 with MB 229.71 or 229.72. Using the wrong oil can affect protection and your service record.

How often should I change my Mercedes oil in UAE heat?

Follow your ASSYST PLUS reminder, which is usually about once a year or every 16,000 km. In heavy traffic or with an AMG, check the oil level often and don’t stretch past the reminder.

Will an oil change reset my service light?

Yes. We reset the ASSYST display with diagnostic software after completing the work, so your next reminder stays accurate.

Is an oil change the same as Service A?

No. Service A includes the oil change plus extra checks and inspections. We can carry out a full Service A or Service B if your car is due.

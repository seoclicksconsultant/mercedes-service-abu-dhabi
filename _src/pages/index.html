Your rights

Your Warranty Stays Protected Outside the Dealer

Servicing outside the dealer does not automatically void your warranty. Under UAE consumer law, updated with new regulations in October 2023, contract terms that harm consumers are void. A dealer cannot cancel your warranty just because a qualified workshop serviced your car with suitable parts.

Our tip: keep every invoice. We give you detailed invoices that list parts, part numbers and oil specifications for exactly this reason.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Mercedes service cost in Abu Dhabi?

It depends on the model, engine and whether it is due for Service A or Service B. Service A costs less than Service B, and AMG or V8 models need more oil and larger parts. We give you a fixed, line-by-line quote after checking your car’s ASSYST data.

How often should I service my Mercedes in UAE heat?

Follow your ASSYST PLUS reminder, which is usually about once a year or every 16,000 km for Service A. In Abu Dhabi, we also recommend a battery, AC and tyre check before every summer, since heat wears these parts faster.

Will servicing outside the dealer void my Mercedes warranty?

Not by itself. UAE rules no longer require dealer-only servicing to keep a valid warranty. Keep detailed invoices that show the parts and oil specification used. We provide these on every job.

Do you use genuine Mercedes-Benz parts?

Yes. We offer genuine Mercedes-Benz parts or OE-quality equivalents from the same suppliers. Your quote shows both options and the price difference, so the choice is yours.

How long does a Mercedes repair take?

Most services and minor repairs are done the same day. Larger jobs such as engine, gearbox or bodywork repairs take longer depending on parts. We give you a time estimate with your quote.

Can you service AMG and EQ electric models?

Yes. We work on AMG performance models and EQ electric Mercedes, including high-voltage system checks, thermal management and software-related faults.

Is 50% tint legal on my Mercedes in Abu Dhabi?

Yes, on the side and rear windows only. Tinting above 50% or tinting without a permit can bring a AED 1,500 fine. We only apply legal tint levels.

## **How Mercedes Bodies Are Built**

A Mercedes body is not a single material. It’s a careful mix chosen for strength and low weight. The 2021 S-Class body, for example, uses more than [50% aluminium](https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2020/09/07/2021-mercedes-s-class-up-to-132-lbs-lighter-more-than-50-aluminum/), combined with high-strength steel in key areas. Each material needs its own repair approach.

| Body area | Typical material | How we repair it |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Bonnet, wings, doors, boot lid (many models) | Aluminium | Aluminium-specific tools, no mixing with steel dust |
| Passenger safety cage | Hot-formed and ultra-high-strength steel | Replaced at approved joints, never heated or straightened |
| Floor and tunnel | Ultra-high-strength and press-hardened steel | Measured and repaired to factory points |
| Front structure (some models) | Cast aluminium | Replaced as specified, not bent back |
| Bumper covers | Plastic | Repaired or replaced, with sensors checked |

Heating high-strength steel can weaken it, and aluminium can corrode if it touches steel particles. This is why a generic body shop approach can quietly reduce crash protection.

## **Why ADAS Calibration Matters After Body Repair**

Modern Mercedes models use cameras, radar and ultrasonic sensors behind the windshield, grille and bumpers. These feed systems such as Distronic, Active Brake Assist and Active Lane Keeping Assist.

Precision matters. If a camera or radar sits even a few degrees off, its view hundreds of metres ahead can land in the wrong lane. Mercedes requires the front camera to be calibrated after a windshield replacement, and radar may need calibration after a bumper is removed and refitted, even if the sensor itself wasn’t damaged.

Most sensors can’t tell you when they’re slightly out of line. That’s why we check and calibrate them as part of the repair, not as an optional extra.

## **Agency vs Non-Agency Insurance Repair**

If your Mercedes is damaged in an accident, your insurance policy decides where it can be repaired. UAE comprehensive policies come with one of two repair options.

|  | Agency repair | Non-agency repair |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Where the car is repaired | The brand’s authorised dealer | An insurer-approved workshop or garage |
| Premium | Higher, usually AED 250 to 600 more per year | Lower |
| Typical speed | Can take longer | Often faster |
| Best for | New cars still under warranty | Older cars and owners wanting faster turnaround |

Under a [non-agency policy](https://www.gargashinsurance.com/faqs/motor-insurance/types-coverage-benefits/what-is-the-difference-between-agency-non-agency-repairs/), your insurer sends the car to an approved workshop. Bring your police report, policy details and any insurer instructions, and we’ll guide you through the next steps. Approval and cover always depend on your insurer and policy.

## **Tips to Avoid Body Damage in Abu Dhabi**

* **Park away from trolley bays:** Mall car parks cause many door dings and bumper scuffs.  
* **Use your sensors and cameras:** Low parking pillars and kerbs are a common cause of bumper damage.  
* **Keep a safe following distance:** Tailgating caused 14% of serious UAE accidents in 2025\.  
* **Stay back from trucks:** Stones and debris from construction vehicles chip paint and crack glass.  
* **Fix chips early:** Bare metal starts to corrode quickly in humid, coastal air.  
* **Consider protection film:** PPF on the bumper and bonnet guards against chips and light scuffs.

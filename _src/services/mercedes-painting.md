### **Repair and Refinish**

These services restore damaged or worn paint to its original colour and gloss.

#### **Scratch and Stone Chip Repair**

Highway sand and stones chip bonnets and bumpers fast. We fill and level each chip, then colour match it to your paint code. Small scratches are often fixed without repainting the whole panel.

#### **Bumper Painting**

Bumpers take most parking scuffs and chips. We repair cracks, prime the plastic with flexible additives and paint it to match. Parking sensors are refitted and tested before handover.

#### **Panel and Door Painting**

When one panel is damaged, we repaint it and blend the colour into the panels beside it. This avoids the visible line you see on cheaper repairs. Door edges and jambs are finished to match.

#### **Full Body Respray**

A full respray suits cars with widespread fading, peeling or many old repairs. We remove trim, sand to a sound base and apply primer, basecoat and clear coat. Your car leaves with an even, factory-like finish.

#### **Paint Correction and Polishing**

Swirls and light scratches often sit only in the clear coat. We measure its thickness first, then machine polish to remove defects. This restores gloss while keeping as much clear coat as possible.

## **Why Your Mercedes Clear Coat Needs Specialist Care**

Mercedes uses a special clear coat that most garages treat like any other. Since late 2003, it has contained ceramic nano-particles that make it three times more scratch-resistant than conventional clear coat. In lab car-wash tests, it also kept about 40% more gloss.

That hardness is a strength on the road, but it changes how repairs must be done. The clear coat itself is thin. Factory clear coat usually measures 35 to 50 microns, and experts advise removing no more than 12.5 microns over the car’s whole life. That is why we measure paint thickness before every polish or repair.

## **How Abu Dhabi Sun Affects Mercedes Paint**

UV light breaks down clear coat over time, and the UAE gets a lot of it. Most of the country has recorded a [UV index of 12](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/lifestyle/health/uae-beware-of-scorching-sun-warns-doctor-as-temperature-soars-past-50-degrees?amp=1) in summer, the “extreme” category. Roofs and bonnets take the worst of it.

Heat adds to the strain. Temperatures have reached 48.1°C in Sweihan near Al Ain, and dark paint gets far hotter than the air. Over several summers, this shows up as fading, chalky clear coat and finally peeling. Covered parking, regular washing and a ceramic coating all slow it down.
